\chapter{Requirements}
\section{Brief}
This chapter defines the requirements that guide the design of revocable consent features for WhatsApp photo sharing. It identifies key stakeholders, outlines realistic use cases, and translates these into functional and non-functional requirements that reflect user, legal and design expectations. The goal is to establish a clear foundation for the prototype by specifying what the system must behave, and the standards it must meet to support intuitive and trustworthy consent revocation.
\section{Stakeholders}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Photo Sender:}
  Must retain ongoing, modifiable consent rights. They need clarity about who can view the ability to modify or revoke access, and a sense of safety and social comfort in revocation.

  \item \textbf{Photo Receiver:}
  They consume the photo and are affected by revocation. They need clear expectations about visibility, fairness in communication, and transparency when access changes. Crucial for privacy, ethics, and contextual integrity.

  \item \textbf{Co-Subjects Depicted in the Photo:}
  This stakeholder is crucial for privacy, ethics, and contextual integrity. They need representation and agency over how they appear in shared content, including mechanisms to request revocation even if they are not the sender, to avoid non-consensual distribution.

  \item \textbf{Secondary Receiver:}
  They enter the system indirectly. This stakeholder is important because misuse typically occurs at this level; the system must contain information flow regardless of user intention. They need clarity about whether they are permitted to view or keep the content, and automatic enforcement of rules (e.g., cannot forward again, cannot save).
\end{enumerate}



\section{Use Cases}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{UC-S0: Sender Applies Permissions Before Sending:}
  Allow the sender to configure photo-sharing permissions before delivery, reducing unwanted redistribution and reliance on later revocation.

  \item \textbf{UC-S1: Sender Manages Ongoing Consent Over Time:}
  Enable the sender to modify or revoke consent after sharing to reflect changing circumstances. All receivers (primary and secondary) are affected by the sender’s changes.

  \item \textbf{UC-R1: Receiver Encounters Controlled or Revoked Access:}
  Ensure the receiver clearly understands restricted, time-limited, or revoked access.

  \item \textbf{UC-C1: Co-Subject Requests Consent Changes from Sender:}
  Allow individuals depicted in a photo to influence sharing decisions.

  \item \textbf{UC-M1: Multiple Co-Subjects with Conflicting Consent:}
  Handle situations where multiple depicted individuals have different consent preferences.

  \item \textbf{UC-C2: Platform Mediated Co-Subject Consent Intervention:}
  Provide a non-interpersonal way for co-subjects to request restriction or removal.

  \item \textbf{UC-SR1: Secondary Receiver Attempts Access or Misuse:}
  Enforce original consent rules when photos are forwarded and a secondary receiver attempts access or misuse.
\end{enumerate}
